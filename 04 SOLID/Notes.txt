1. Number of classes increases = Coupling between classes increases
    Which leads to
    |- Maintenability issues
    |- Readability problems
    |- Increased bugs and debugging efforts
    |- Monetary losses

2. Robert C. Martin - 
Published the SOLID design principles in 2000, which are widely regarded as best practices for maintainable and scalable object-oriented designs.

3. SOLID Acronym
    |- Single Responsibility Principle (SRP)
    |- Open-Closed Principle (OCP)
    |- Liskov Substitution Principle (LSP)
    |- Interface Segregation Principle (ISP)
    |- Dependency Inversion Principle (DIP)
